3. The 100 DOTS School ERP

When a school uses our software, the school decides what student, parent and staff information is stored. The school is responsible for that information (the "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). We store and process it only on the school's behalf and under a written agreement with the school, to provide the service.

Parents and students who want to see, correct or delete their information should contact their school first. We will help the school respond.
