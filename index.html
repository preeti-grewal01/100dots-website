100 DOTS SCHOOL ERP

Everything a school needs

Over 20 modules covering daily school management. One system, three platforms.

👨‍🎓

Student management

Student profiles, enrolment, promotion, transfer certificates and academic year tracking.

📋

Assignments and homework

Create, assign and grade assignments. Students submit online with file attachments.

✅

Attendance

Daily attendance with Present, Absent and Late. Weekly and monthly reports with percentages.

💰

Fee management

Fee structures, instalments, late fees, payment recording and PDF receipts, with cashier sessions.

📢

Circulars

Class-wise circulars with attachments. Mark notices urgent and track views.

📊

Report cards

Draft, submit, authorise and publish. PDF download for parents with a QR code to verify authenticity.

👩‍🏫

Staff management

Staff profiles, attendance, leave tracking and role-based access.

💬

Communication

Messaging between teachers and students, with inbox, unread badges and reply threads.

⏰

Timetables

Upload and manage class timetables, with the newest one highlighted.

🖼️

School gallery

Share event photos with everyone in the school on web and mobile.

📈

Reports and analytics

Attendance reports, fee summaries and submission tracking.

📱

Android app

A full mobile app for teachers, students and admins on Android phones.
